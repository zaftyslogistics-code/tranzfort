Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026 · Terms of Service

TranZfort Technologies (“TranZfort”, “we”) operates a freight introduction marketplace connecting suppliers and truckers in India. We do not arrange payments, escrow, or delivery guarantees in the app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name, mobile number, email, role (supplier/trucker), language preference.
  • Verification (optional): profile photo, Aadhaar/PAN document images, last four digits of Aadhaar, masked PAN tail, business details for suppliers, truck documents for truckers.
  • Marketplace: load postings, booking requests, messages, ratings, operating city/location when you provide it.
  • Device: push notification tokens, crash/diagnostic logs (no full government ID numbers in logs).

2. How we use data

To provide marketplace listings, platform-access review, safety reports, notifications you opt into, and legal compliance. We do not sell personal data.

3. Retention (summary)

  • Active account: profile and marketplace data while your account is active.
  • Verification documents: retained for review and audit until account deletion or as required by law; full Aadhaar numbers are not stored in the profile table after v1 minimization.
  • Deleted account: profile marked for deletion; verification, truck, and profile-photo storage prefixes purged on confirmed deletion request.
  • Legal holds: longer retention only where required by applicable law.

4. Sharing

We use cloud infrastructure (e.g. Supabase) under contract. Counterparties see only marketplace-safe fields (name, ratings, platform-review status)—not your government ID images or full ID numbers.

5. Your rights (India — DPDP Act)

You may access, correct, or delete your account via in-app settings. Contact us to exercise grievance or nomination rights.
